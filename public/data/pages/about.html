Why we exist.

Mission Statement

Luna Goco Colleges shall provide opportunities for education and training in the students’ chosen fields, developing them into active citizens who contribute to improving the quality of life—dedicated to the service of God and the community.

Vision

To be a source of holistic educational experiences, enabling students to lead lives of choice, purpose, service, and integrity as well-rounded individuals.

PHILOSOPHY

The school accepts as its primary functions the pursuit of education, research, and service to humanity. Based on these, the following statements of philosophy of life and education have been established:

1. God created man in His image.

2. Man is a rational, social, and spiritual being composed of body and soul, endowed with intellect, and therefore capable of seeking, attaining, and enjoying truth.

3. A society with democratic values is dynamic and composed of people with varying ideologies and cultures, who must be treated equally regardless of race, creed, socio-economic status, or political beliefs..

4. Education is a continuous, self-realizing process that embraces all experiences through which a person assimilates knowledge, develops his or her potential, and establishes moral values, enabling an understanding and appreciation of the purpose of life.

5. Students are unique individuals with intellectual, physical, emotional, spiritual, and social needs, and the educational program should provide for these needs.

6. Students learn best through participation in a friendly, democratic environment where all individuals involved share in the accomplishment of goals. God created man in His image.

7. The curriculum endeavors to promote self-activity through learning experiences that include reflective thinking, perceptual experiences, and problem-solving.
