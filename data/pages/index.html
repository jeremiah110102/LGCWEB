Why LGC

A small campus with a wide reach.

We keep our colleges close to industry and our classes small enough that no one goes unnoticed.

01 — Faculty

Practitioners, not just lecturers

Most instructors hold active licenses or run practices alongside teaching, so coursework tracks what the field actually needs.

02 — Outcomes

Board exams and job placement

Review classes are built into the curriculum in the graduating year, and our career office works placements from third year onward.

03 — Community

Rooted in Oriental Mindoro

Extension programs, local partnerships, and city-based fieldwork keep the college tied to the community it was built to serve.
