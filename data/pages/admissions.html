Admissions

Step 1

Submit requirements

Bring or upload your credentials to the admissions office or portal.

Step 2

Take the entrance exam

Held every Saturday on campus. Results are released within five working days.

Step 3

Interview

A short conversation with the program coordinator — about fit, not just scores.

Step 4

Enroll

Settle initial fees and register for classes at the registrar's office.

Requirements

Incoming freshmen

  • Original and photocopy of Form 138 (report card)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • PSA-issued birth certificate
  • 2×2 ID photo, white background (2 copies)
  • Completed entrance exam application form

Requirements

Transferees

  • Official Transcript of Records or copy in progress
  • Honorable Dismissal from previous school
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • PSA-issued birth certificate
  • 2×2 ID photo, white background (2 copies)
